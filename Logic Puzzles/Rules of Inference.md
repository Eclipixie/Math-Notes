* Modus Ponens ($\text{M.P.}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta$
	* $\alpha$
	* $\therefore \beta$ 
* Addition ($\text{Add.}$):
	* $\alpha$
	* $\therefore \alpha \lor \beta$ 
* Hypothetical Syllogism ($\text{H.S.}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta$
	* $\beta \Rightarrow \gamma$ 
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \gamma$ 
* Modus Tollens ($\text{M.T.}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta$ 
	* $\lnot \beta$
	* $\therefore \lnot \alpha$ 
* Constructive Dilemma ($\text{C.D.}$):
	* $(\alpha \Rightarrow \beta) \land (\gamma \Rightarrow \delta)$ 
	* $\alpha \lor \gamma$
	* $\therefore \beta \lor \delta$ 
* Disjunctive Syllogism ($\text{D.S.}$):
	* $\alpha \lor \beta$
	* $\lnot \alpha$
	* $\therefore \beta$
* Absorption ($\text{Abs.}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta$
	* $\therefore \alpha \Rightarrow (\alpha \land \beta)$ 
* Conjunction ($\text{Conj.}$):
	* $\alpha$
	* $\beta$
	* $\therefore \alpha \land \beta$
* Simplification ($\text{Simp.}$):
	* $\alpha \land \beta$ 
	* $\therefore \alpha$
