* Commutative Law ($\text{comm}$):
	* $\alpha \land \beta=\beta \land \alpha$
	* $\alpha \lor \beta=\beta \lor \alpha$
* Associative Law ($\text{assoc}$):
	* $\alpha \land (\beta \land \gamma)=(\alpha \land \beta) \land \gamma$ 
	* $\alpha \lor (\beta \lor \gamma)=(\alpha \lor \beta) \lor \gamma$ 
* Transposition ($\text{trans}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta = \lnot \beta \Rightarrow \lnot \alpha$ 
* Material Implication ($\text{imp}$):
	* $\alpha \Rightarrow \beta = \lnot \alpha \Rightarrow \beta$ 
* Material Equivocation ($\text{equiv}$):
	* $\alpha \Leftrightarrow \beta = (\alpha \Rightarrow \beta) \land (\beta \Rightarrow \alpha) = (\alpha \land \beta) \lor (\lnot \alpha \lor \lnot \beta)$
* Distributive Law ($\text{dist}$)
	* $\alpha \land (\beta \lor \gamma) = (\alpha \land \beta) \lor (\alpha \land \gamma)$ 
	* $\alpha \lor (\beta \land \gamma) = (\alpha \lor \beta) \land (\alpha \lor \gamma)$ 
* De Morgan's Theorem ($\text{DeM}$):
	* $\lnot (\alpha \land \beta) = \lnot \alpha \lor \lnot \beta$ 
	* $\lnot (\alpha \lor \beta) = \lnot \alpha \land \lnot \beta$ 
* Exportation ($\text{exp}$):
	* $(\alpha \land \beta) \Rightarrow \gamma = \alpha \Rightarrow (\beta \Rightarrow \gamma)$ 
* Double Negation ($\text{dn}$):
	* $\lnot \lnot \alpha = \alpha$
* Tautology ($\text{taut}$):
	* $\alpha = \alpha \land \alpha = \alpha \lor \alpha$
